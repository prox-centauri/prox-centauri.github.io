About

Substantive Hip-Hop, layered writing, and a deliberate focus on lyricism without forced excess.

Flint, Michigan born lyricist Prox Centauri is intent on melding his love of intellectualism and high-level linguistics to craft music that expresses his passionate drive for substantive Hip-Hop. Heavily inspired by a myriad of artistic and academic mediums, the expansive emcee explores various topics through a litany of complex styles and schemes. Influenced by artists such as Nas, AZ, MF DOOM, eIZHi, Big L, Twista, and Ras Kass, Prox aims to utilize his creative energies to forward the importance of lyricism.
